## Biography

I am a population geneticist and computational biologist currently working as a Postdoctoral Fellow at Uppsala University, affiliated with the **Centre for the Human Past** and the **Swedish Collegium for Advanced Study (SCAS)** through the STINT and Human Past Fellowships. 

My research investigates human evolutionary history, ancient demographic history, and genetic diversity in South Asian populations. I earned my Ph.D. in Zoology (Population Genetics) from Mangalore University.

---

## Research Focus

My work bridges population genomics, ancient DNA analysis, and historical linguistics to understand human migratory waves and demographic dynamics:

* **Proto-Dravidian & Indigenous Lineages:** Identifying deep ancestral components (~4,400 years old) shared across Dravidian-speaking populations and indigenous groups like the Koraga tribe.
* **Socio-Cultural Structure & Genetics:** Examining how endogamy, clan systems (such as *gotra* in Koṅkaṅī Sārasvata Brahmins), and traditional occupations shape paternal (Y-STR/SNP) and maternal (mtDNA) genetic architecture.
* **Island & Coastal Demographics:** Uncovering founder events, post-glacial migrations, and genetic continuity in coastal and archipelago populations (e.g., Lakshadweep and the southwestern coast of India).
* **Precision Medicine Applications:** Constructing comprehensive genetic catalogs of endogamous groups to inform regional sampling strategies and precision medicine.
