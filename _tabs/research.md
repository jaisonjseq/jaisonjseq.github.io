## Overview

My research focuses on South Asian population genetics, investigating ancient demographic history, deep maternal and paternal lineages, and genetic continuity across endogamous groups.

### Core Research Areas

* **Proto-Dravidian Genetic Ancestry:** Investigating an ancient ~4,400-year-old genetic component shared across Dravidian-speaking populations and indigenous groups like the Koraga.
* **Maternal & Paternal Lineages:** Analyzing mitogenome diversity (such as the U1 haplogroup) and Y-STR variation to map post-glacial migrations, gotra systems, and occupation-based population structures.
* **Archipelago & Coastal Demographics:** Reconstructing genetic footprints and single-founding events in regions like the Lakshadweep archipelago and the southwest coast of India.
* **Bioinformatics & Ancient DNA:** Implementing high-throughput pipelines (e.g., PLINK, ADMIXTOOLS, DATES, BEAST2) to infer demographic history and support precision medicine sampling.
